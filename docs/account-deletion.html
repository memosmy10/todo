Send an account deletion request

  1. Email [REPLACE WITH YOUR MONITORED SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address registered to your Daylist account.
  2. Use the subject Daylist account deletion. In the message, ask us to permanently delete your Daylist account and associated data.
  3. Do not include your password. We may reply to verify that you control the account before completing the request.

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days and will contact you if additional verification is needed. Deletion removes your Supabase Auth account and the tasks linked to it. If we must retain information for a legal reason, we will explain what must be retained and why.

Sending an email opens your email app and does not submit anything until you send the message.
