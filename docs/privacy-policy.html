Information we handle

When you create a Daylist account, you provide your name, email address, and password. Supabase Authentication processes and verifies your sign-in credentials; the app does not store your password in its task database. When you use Daylist, it stores the task text you add, each task’s completion status and creation time, and the account identifier associated with those tasks.

Daylist does not request device location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Why information is used

  • Your email and password are used to create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • Your name is used to greet you in the app.
  • Your tasks and completion status are used to show your list and synchronize it to your account.
  • Account information may be used to respond to requests you send to our support contact.

Retention and account deletion

Your account and tasks are retained while your account remains active. You can start permanent deletion from the app’s Home screen using Delete account, or without reinstalling the app by following the instructions on our account deletion page. Deleting an account removes its Supabase Auth account and associated tasks. A deletion request sent to support will be verified and processed within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law. Children

Daylist is a general to-do list and is not designed to collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided information to us, contact the privacy email above so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
