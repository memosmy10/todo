Create your Daylist account

Account creation and your personal task list are handled securely in the Daylist app. The email confirmation helps us verify your email address before sign-in.

  1. Open Daylist below. If you don’t have it installed yet, install it from Google Play when it is available.
  2. Choose Create account, then enter your name, email, password, and password confirmation.
  3. Check your inbox and confirm the email on the same device where you created the account. Daylist then opens your task list. If you finish confirmation elsewhere, open Daylist and sign in with the same email and password.
