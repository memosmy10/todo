## Information the app handles

When you create an account, Daylist processes the name and email address you provide. Supabase Authentication handles your password; Daylist does not put passwords in its task table. Daylist stores the tasks you create, including their completion status and creation time, together with your account identifier.

## How information is used

This information is used to create and secure your account, show your task list, and synchronize your tasks between sessions. Daylist does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Storage and service providers

Account and task data are processed by the Supabase project configured by the app publisher. Review and disclose the project's hosting region and applicable Supabase service terms before publishing. Data is sent to Supabase over HTTPS. The app's database policies restrict each account to its own tasks.

## Retention and deletion

Your information is retained while your account is active. You can request deletion using **Delete account** in the app. The request deletes the Supabase Auth account, and the task table's account relationship removes that account's tasks. If deletion fails, contact [Add a monitored support email address] to request help.

## Children and changes

Daylist is not designed to collect information from children. This policy may be updated as the app or its services change; the published copy should include its effective date and a way to contact the publisher.
